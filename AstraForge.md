# AstraForge

**The open mission operations stack for aerospace innovation.**

AstraForge is an open-source mission operations platform designed to provide the infrastructure needed to plan, simulate, monitor, and operate rockets, spacecraft, satellites, and exploration systems.

Built under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, AstraForge provides a transparent, modular foundation for aerospace organizations, researchers, universities, and open space communities to build the next generation of mission systems.

---

# Overview

AstraForge combines mission control, telemetry intelligence, digital twin simulation, spacecraft operations, ground communications, and aerospace data infrastructure into a unified open-source platform.

The goal of AstraForge is to create an open mission operations stack that enables:

- Rocket launch management
- Spacecraft operations
- Satellite constellation management
- Mission simulation
- Space communications
- Earth observation workflows
- Human spaceflight support
- Orbital awareness
- Collaborative aerospace innovation
