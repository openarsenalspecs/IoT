# AeroGrid

**Predicting Tomorrow's Storms, Today.**

AeroGrid is an open source, modular platform for autonomous atmospheric observation using AI-powered drones capable of performing and expanding upon the traditional operations of weather balloons. The platform combines reusable high-altitude aircraft, advanced environmental sensors, self-driving navigation technologies, and real-time weather forecasting to create a distributed network of intelligent atmospheric monitoring systems.

Released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, AeroGrid is designed as a community-driven ecosystem of open hardware and open software that enables researchers, universities, governments, citizen scientists, and organizations to build, modify, and deploy autonomous weather observation platforms without vendor lock-in.

---

# Vision

To create a globally distributed network of autonomous weather drones that continuously monitor the atmosphere, improve forecasting accuracy, provide early warning capabilities, and make atmospheric science accessible through open source technology.
