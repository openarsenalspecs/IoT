# TerraGuild AI Specification

## Overview

TerraGuild AI is an open modular artificial intelligence specification for designing, visualizing, and managing regenerative landscapes, food forests, tree guilds, permaculture systems, and ecological food production networks.

The specification defines a framework for combining generative AI, ecological knowledge systems, geographic analysis, botanical intelligence, biological modeling, and environmental simulation to create resilient landscapes that support human food production while improving biodiversity, soil health, and ecosystem stability.

TerraGuild AI enables individuals, communities, farms, and organizations to transform land information, population requirements, climate conditions, and ecological goals into complete regenerative landscape plans.

The system is designed to support everything from residential food forests to large-scale community food systems.

# Modular Architecture

TerraGuild AI is built as a collection of independent but interoperable modules.

Each module can operate independently or combine into a complete ecological intelligence platform.

Core shared systems include:

- Ecological knowledge graph
- Botanical intelligence database
- GIS integration
- Climate intelligence
- Generative AI engine
- 3D visualization engine
- Environmental simulation engine
- Recommendation engine
- Human feedback system
