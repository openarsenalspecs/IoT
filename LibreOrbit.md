# LibreOrbit

## Connect Beyond Limits

LibreOrbit is an open-source, modular satellite networking operating system designed to connect satellites, ground stations, sensors, edge devices, and communities through open, interoperable, and decentralized space infrastructure.

Licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, LibreOrbit provides a flexible foundation for satellite communications, mission operations, data exchange, autonomous systems, and future space networks.

LibreOrbit is designed around a plugin-based architecture where every capability can operate as an independent module. Organizations, researchers, communities, and developers can deploy only the components they need while contributing improvements back to the ecosystem.

---
