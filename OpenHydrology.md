# OpenHydrology

**Designed to Replace, Not Retrofit.**

OpenHydrology is an open-source, modular water purification and management platform designed to move beyond traditional reverse osmosis systems. It uses selective electro-separation, intelligent automation, open hardware, and community-driven development to create a transparent, repairable, and scalable water infrastructure system.

Instead of removing everything from water and attempting to rebuild it afterward, OpenHydrology focuses on selectively removing unwanted contaminants while preserving beneficial minerals and enabling controlled remineralization profiles inspired by natural spring systems.

OpenHydrology is designed for homes, off-grid systems, RVs, communities, municipalities, research institutions, and future decentralized water networks.

---

# Project Vision

Modern water treatment systems often rely on proprietary filters, sealed components, and black-box technologies. OpenHydrology creates an alternative based on open standards, modular engineering, and public collaboration.

The goal is to create a global open-source water platform that enables communities to:

- Understand their water
- Improve their water quality
- Build and repair their own systems
- Share improvements openly
- Reduce dependence on proprietary infrastructure
