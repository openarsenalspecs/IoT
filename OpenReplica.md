# OpenReplica

## Build once. Never be trapped again.

OpenReplica is a free and open-source platform that lets anyone replicate websites in seconds and transform them into clean, portable, self-owned projects.

No subscriptions.  
No proprietary lock-in.  
No export paywalls.  
No artificial restrictions.

If it runs in a browser, you should be able to own your version of it.

OpenReplica is built for creators, developers, educators, archivists, small businesses, and anyone tired of being trapped inside closed website ecosystems.

Licensed under the **GNU AGPL 3.0+** and developed openly on GitLab.

---

# Why OpenReplica Exists

Too many creators spend years building websites they never truly own.

Monthly subscriptions become dependency.  
Migration becomes expensive.  
Exporting becomes intentionally difficult.  
Platforms control the experience instead of the user.

OpenReplica restores digital ownership by making websites:

- Portable
- Editable
- Self-hostable
- Transparent
- Future-proof

The mission is simple:

> Build once. Never be trapped again.
