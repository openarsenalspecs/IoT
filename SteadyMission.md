# SteadyMission

**Where Data Honors Direction.**

SteadyMission is an open-source AI simulation and mission-alignment platform that helps organizations achieve business objectives without violating their mission, values, operational principles, or long-term purpose.

The platform transforms mission statements into structured decision constraints, simulates strategic outcomes, and evaluates whether proposed actions remain aligned with the organization's stated direction.

SteadyMission is designed for businesses, nonprofits, cooperatives, educational institutions, healthcare organizations, governments, and mission-driven teams seeking transparent, explainable, and accountable AI-assisted strategy modeling.

---

## 🧠 Modular Multi-Agent AI Architecture

SteadyMission is built around a modular, service-oriented multi-agent architecture. Every agent operates as an independent component with a clearly defined responsibility, allowing organizations to enable only the capabilities they need or extend the platform with custom agents. Agents collaborate through a shared reasoning pipeline, where each evaluates a proposed strategy from its own perspective before contributing to a final recommendation.

The architecture is designed to be transparent and explainable. Rather than relying on a single AI model to produce an answer, SteadyMission encourages structured collaboration, debate, validation, and evidence-based reasoning between specialized agents.

# Shared Core Services

Every core module communicates through shared platform services.

### Mission Constraint Engine
Transforms mission statements into structured decision constraints used throughout the platform.

### Knowledge Retrieval Engine
Retrieves relevant organizational documents, policies, procedures, and historical decisions using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

### Decision Orchestrator
Coordinates agent execution order, collects findings, and produces final recommendations.

### Consensus Engine
Calculates agreement scores, identifies conflicting opinions, and determines confidence levels.

### Explainability Engine
Produces transparent reasoning reports showing how every recommendation was reached.

### Mission Fidelity Scoring
Evaluates every strategy based on:
- Mission alignment
- Ethical consistency
- Financial viability
- Operational feasibility
- Long-term sustainability
- Organizational trust
